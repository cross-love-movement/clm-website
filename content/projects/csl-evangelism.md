In Ghana, the places where young people gather can shape them more than the classroom or the pew — for good and for harm. Football pitches, basketball courts, clubhouses, and game centres become a second home.

Christo-Socio Lingo is our attempt to carry Christian values from the church and the home into those spaces.

We learn the “lingo” of each place — its rules, policies, and culture — then connect them to Biblical case studies using creatively designed tracts.

The aim is common ground: a place where Christian values show up inside the world young people already know, so they can relate, belong, and connect. The engagement also opens the door to mentoring younger members.
