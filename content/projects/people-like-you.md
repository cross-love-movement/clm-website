As April 2028 approaches — ten years of community engagement — we have been asking what we can give to the youth of this generation.

People Like You is our answer: a one-day, in-person youth empowerment and capacity-building symposium on 12 June 2027 in the Tema enclave.

It brings together 700 to 1,000 young people across junior and secondary school, tertiary education, and job-seeking for mentorship and strategic guidance from locally and internationally renowned young mentors.

The aim is awareness, practical guidance, and confidence — helping young people make informed decisions and stay focused on their ambitions, so the next generation becomes a strong and cohesive force for Ghana’s sustainable development.
