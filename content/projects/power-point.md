After the creative showcases at Cross Sessions, we ask one question: what is the one-point lesson our audience can take home from this edition?

Power-Point is where we host youth mentors and creative leads for exhortations, panel discussions, and presentations that equip the audience for impact in their communities.

At the 7th edition in 2025, the BusStop Boyz — young professionals tackling sanitation in Ghana through volunteerism — inspired young people from different denominations to start in their own homes and churches.

At the 8th edition in 2026, Edward Asare, a creative mentor and youth leader from First Baptist Church, Tema, spoke on “Christ, Creatives, Church, and Country”, encouraging Christian creatives to anchor their work in Christ.
