In Bawku, a community has confronted tribal conflict for more than two decades. The local church has remained one of the few places people can gather for comfort and hope.

During the 4th edition of Cross Sessions, we raised funds and bought twenty chairs for the Bakanga Pentecost Church.

Since the donation, the congregation has seen growth in membership and participation — a small piece of infrastructure making room for a community that needs one.
