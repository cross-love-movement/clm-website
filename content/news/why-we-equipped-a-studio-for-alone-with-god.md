Some projects begin with a need so plain it is almost easy to miss: a team doing good work with the wrong equipment.

Alone With God Devotional is a group of young Christians who write and design devotions for young people, aimed at moral and holistic development. The writing was never the constraint. The production was.

At the 5th edition of Cross Sessions, we raised funds to set up an editing studio for the team.

They have continued to publish since — and remain one of our support partners. It is the kind of project we look for: an existing team, a specific gap, and a result that keeps working after the handover.
