As April 2028 approaches — ten years since the Movement began — we have been asking one question: what can we give to the youth of this generation?

The answer is **People Like You**, a one-day, in-person youth empowerment and capacity-building symposium on **12 June 2027** in the Tema enclave.

It will bring together 700 to 1,000 young people from junior and secondary school, tertiary education, and job-seeking. The day pairs them with locally and internationally renowned young mentors for practical guidance on career, social, and personal decisions.

The aim is awareness, practical guidance, and confidence: helping young people make informed decisions and stay focused on their ambitions, so the next generation becomes a strong and cohesive force for Ghana’s sustainable development.
